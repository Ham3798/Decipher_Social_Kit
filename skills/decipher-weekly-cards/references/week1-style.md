# Week1 Style Reference

Use the established week1 exported cards as the visual baseline. Do not encode a personal local path in reusable skill instructions.

## Global Style

- Canvas: Instagram portrait, 1080x1350.
- Background: warm paper texture feel, off-white beige base, subtle grain and faint Decipher watermark.
- Typography: restrained black and gray, large Korean display text, no decorative gradients.
- Framing: photos sit in a white matte frame with soft shadow.
- Match the actual week1 exported PNG coordinates, not just current code defaults:
  - Weekly photo frame outer box: `x=80`, `y=86`, `w=920`, `h=716`.
  - Speaker photo frame outer box: `x=107`, `y=174`, `w=866`, `h=618`.
  - Interview cover photo frame outer box: `x=170`, `y=86`, `w=740`, `h=706`.
- Logos: Decipher logo appears as a small anchor, not a dominant decoration.
- Mood: editorial, quiet, archival, academic. Avoid loud social-media stickers, emojis, bright colors, or dense badges.

## Weekly Card

- Use one main collage near the top.
- For two landscape weekly photos, stack them top/bottom inside the week1 frame.
- For two portrait-heavy photos, use left/right columns so each subject has enough height.
- Title sits in the lower half, large and left-aligned.
- Author line uses `by 이름1, 이름2`.
- Date/session line is smaller and light gray.

## Speaker Card

- Put the Decipher mark near the top left.
- Place the speaker/session photo high on the card in a white frame.
- Use a dark vertical or side tag only when a tag/handle exists.
- Speaker name is very large at the lower left.
- Title/affiliation is below the name, then a short divider line, then date/session.
- If there is no speaker session, use the established blank speaker card convention with the date only.

## Interview Cover

- Photo is high and centered in a white frame.
- For strongly vertical portraits, use blur-fill plus original-image contain inside the frame instead of cropping the person. Keep the treatment subtle so it looks like photo handling, not decoration.
- Keep interview label small and spaced out.
- Name is large in the lower left.
- Instagram handle is below the name; use `@decipher_global` there when no Instagram handle is available.
- X/Twitter handle is used for the dark photo-side tag when provided.
- Decipher wordmark anchors the lower right.
- Use a dark tag near the photo only if it does not cover the subject.

## Interview Detail Slides

- Use a cream inner panel on the paper background.
- Match the actual week1 exported slide visually: centered bold title near `62px`, body near `36px semibold`, and compact Korean line rhythm.
- Treat those sizes as the spacious default. Use only a few bounded whole-block tiers for unusually long titles or answers, and keep the body above the renderer's readability floor.
- Use a thin divider line below the title.
- Preserve meaningful paragraph breaks and uniform body typography. Do not create word-level size changes or decorative emphasis.
- Keep Decipher logo small at the lower right.

## Copy Tone

- Korean should be factual and polished while preserving supplied interview content and voice.
- Prefer final title copy over explanatory copy.
- Avoid saying the card “shows,” “contains,” or “features.”
- Summarize only when the user explicitly permits it; otherwise correct spelling, spacing, and sentence flow without dropping substance.

## Avoid

- Heavy crop that removes faces, slides, or context.
- One-color purple, blue, beige-only, or trendy gradient treatments.
- Extra cards inside cards beyond the white photo frame or detail panel.
- Decorative orbs, stickers, emojis, or generic marketing phrases.
- Unstructured text blocks without deliberate paragraphs or adequate line rhythm.
